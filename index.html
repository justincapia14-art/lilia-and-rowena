Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our hardwood, bespoke sizing, and white-glove delivery.

What wood species do you use for your furniture?
We exclusively work with kiln-dried, seasoned Philippine and imported hardwoods including Mahogany, Teak, Ash, and Narra. We NEVER use particleboard, hollow veneer cores, or low-grade MDF that deteriorate under Philippine humidity.
How does custom dimension ordering work?
When you select any customizable piece, you can specify your target Length, Width, Height, and wood stain finish. Our system calculates the material surcharge transparently, and our master carpenters construct it according to your exact room floorplan.
What are your payment and downpayment terms?
We operate on a secure 50% downpayment model to source your selected hardwood and assign master carpenters. The remaining 50% balance is payable in Cash on Delivery (COD) or online transfer only after your piece has arrived and passed your in-home inspection.
How long does custom production and delivery take?
Catalog items in stock are dispatched within 3 to 5 business days. Bespoke pieces with customized dimensions take 10 to 14 business days to allow proper wood curing, assembly, and 3-coat hand-rubbed protective finishing.
What is covered under your 5-Year Structural Warranty?
All our furniture is backed by a 5-Year Structural Warranty covering timber warping, joinery splits, and frame integrity. If any joint becomes loose under normal domestic use, our workshop technician will repair or replace it free of charge.

Step-by-Step Setup in XAMPP & phpMyAdmin:

  1. Extract to htdocs: Extract the downloaded ZIP archive into C:\xampp\htdocs\lilia_and_rowena_furniture\.
  2. Start XAMPP: Open the XAMPP Control Panel and start both Apache and MySQL.
  3. Import SQL Database: Open http://localhost/phpmyadmin, click the Import tab, and import database/01_furniture_shop_schema.sql first, followed by database/02_sample_data.sql.
  4. Run Composer (Optional): Run composer dump-autoload in the project root terminal. (A native PSR-4 fallback autoloader is also included in public/index.php so it works out of the box even without Composer).
  5. Open in Browser: Visit http://localhost/lilia_and_rowena_furniture/public/.
